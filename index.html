⚡ 2-Qism: Say vs Tell & Reporting Verbs Decision Lab (15 ta Savol)
Asosiy qoida: say (that) = kimgaligi aytilmaydi (He said that...). tell + someone (that) = kimgaligi shart (He told me that...). Maxsus fe'llar: remind s.o to V1, refuse to V1, promise to V1 / that, admit/deny + V-ing.
💡 O'zbekcha qopqon: O'zbek tilida "U aytdi" deb kimgaligini aytmasak ham bo'ladi, lekin ingliz tilida "He told that..." ❌ bo'lmaydi! Har doim "He told ME that..." yoki "He said that..." ✅ bo'lishi shart!
🔄 3-Qism: Direct Speech dan Reported Speech ga O'girish (15 ta Savol)

🎯 5-Qism: Reported Speech Integratsiyalashgan Test (10 ta Test)

✍️ 6-Qism: Kontekstual Tarjima (8 ta Gap)
O'zbek tilidagi fikrlarni ingliz tilidagi o'zlashtirma gap qoidalari asosida professional tarjima qiling.
📝 7-Qism: Suhbatni Hikoya Qilib Yozish (Mini-Insho)

Topshiriq: Kechagi suhbatni qayta hikoya qilib bering (8-10 gap)

Yaqinda do'stingiz, shifokor yoki ustozingiz bilan bo'lib o'tgan qiziqarli suhbatni eslang. Ushbu suhbatni to'liq Reported Speech orqali hikoya qiling.

Talablar: Kamida 4 xil reporting verbs (said that, told me that, asked if/whether, promised to/that, explained that, refused to) va vaqt ko'rsatkichlari (the previous day, the next day, that day) ishlatilsin.
