Future in the Past & Reported Speech Mastery

O'tmishdan kelajakka qarash (was/were going to, would), to'g'ri nutqni o'zlashtirma nutqqa aylantirish (Reported Speech), zamonlarning orqaga surilishi (Backshift), vaqt va olmoshlar o'zgarishi hamda savol va buyruq jumlalarining murakkab transformatsiyasi.

🔊 1-Qism: Intention & Reporting Vocabulary (12 ta So'z)
Tugmachani bosib so'zlarning to'g'ri talaffuzini eshiting va ularning o'zlashtirma nutqda qo'llanishiga e'tibor bering.
⚡ 2-Qism: Future in the Past Decision Lab (15 ta Savol)
Qoida: was/were going to = o'tmishda qilinmoqchi bo'lgan, lekin ko'pincha amalga oshmagan niyat/reja. would = o'tmishda turib kelajak haqida berilgan va'da yoki bashorat (will ning o'tgan zamoni).
💡 Asosiy farq: "I was going to call you, but my phone battery died." (Niyat bor edi, uzilib qoldi). "He told me that he would love me forever." (O'tmishdagi va'da/kelajak).
🔄 3-Qism: To'g'ri Nutqni O'zlashtirma Nutqqa Aylantirish (15 ta Transformatsiya)
Berilgan to'g'ri nutqdagi gapni o'zlashtirma nutqqa (Reported Speech) o'giring. Zamonlar orqaga surilishi (backshift), olmoshlar va vaqt ko'rsatkichlariga qat'iy e'tibor bering.
🔍 4-Qism: Xatolarni Topish va To'g'rilash (10 ta Qopqon)
Reported Speech va Future in the Past qoidalarining buzilishi. Gapni to'liq to'g'rilab yozing.
🎯 5-Qism: Maxsus Reporting Fe'llar Testi (10 ta Savol)
say / tell o'rniga admit, warn, refuse, deny, accuse, recommend, threaten kabi fe'llarning to'g'ri sintaktik grammatik modelini tanlang.
✍️ 6-Qism: Kontekstual Tarjima (8 ta Gap)
O'zbek tilidagi murakkab gaplarni Future in the Past va Reported Speech qoidalariga rioya qilib tarjima qiling.
📝 7-Qism: Hikoya Yozish Labi: "The Day When Nothing Went According to Plan"
Boshlangan rejalaringiz buzilib ketgan yoki kutilmagan xabarlar olgan bir kun haqida inglizcha hikoya yozing. Matnda kamida 3 ta 'was/were going to', 2 ta 'would' va 3 ta Reported Speech (someone said / told / warned) jumlalari qatnashishi shart!

Mavzu: The Day When Nothing Went According to Plan (Rejalar o'zgargan kun)

Masalan: "I was going to catch the 8 AM train, but my alarm didn't ring. My friend called and told me that the meeting had been canceled..."
