Modal Verbs: Can, Could, May, Might & 30 Noto'g'ri Fe'l (61–90)

Qobiliyat (Ability), Ehtimollik (Possibility), Ruxsat (Permission), Iltimos (Requests) va Mantiqiy xulosa (Deduction) o'rtasidagi nozik farqlar hamda B1 darajasidagi 30 ta noto'g'ri fe'llar interaktiv trenajyori.

🎯 1. ABILITY (Qobiliyat)
Hozirgi va o'tmishdagi jismoniy/aqliy qobiliyat:
• CAN: "I can swim." (hozir qila olaman)
• COULD: "When I was 5, I could swim." (o'tmishdagi qobiliyat)
• May va might qobiliyat uchun aslo ishlatilmaydi!
🔮 2. POSSIBILITY (Ehtimollik: may, might, could)
Kelajak yoki hozirgi noaniq ehtimol (~50% yoki undan kam):
• "It may / might rain later." (balki yog'ishi mumkin)
• "He could be in the library." (kutubxonada bo'lishi ham mumkin)
• Inkor: might not / may not (balki ...masdir).
🤝 3. PERMISSION & REQUESTS (Ruxsat va Iltimos)
Muloyimlik darajalari:
• Can I...? (norasmiy) | Could I...? (muloyim) | May I...? (rasmiy)
• Iltimos: "Could you please help me?"
• Ruxsat berish: "Yes, you can / may" (lekin you could ❌ XATO).
🕵️ 4. DEDUCTION (Mantiqiy xulosa: can't vs may/might)
Mantiqan mutlaqo imkonsiz holat:
• "He can't be poor — he drives a Ferrari!" (bo'lishi aslo mumkin emas)
• "That story can't be true!" (haqiqat bo'lishi imkonsiz)
⚠️ O'ZBEKCHA QOPQON (Eng ko'p qilinadigan xatolar):
O'zbek tilida "mumkin" so'zi qobiliyat (suza olaman), ehtimol (yog'ishi mumkin) va ruxsat (kirish mumkin) uchun bir xil ishlatiladi. Ingliz tilida esa:
1. Qobiliyat uchun FAQAT can / could (may swim ❌).
2. "May you help me?" ❌ EMAS, balki "Could you help me?" ✅ deb iltimos qilinadi.
3. Mantiqiy imkonsiz narsaga "may not" emas, qat'iy "can't" (He can't be at home!) ishlatiladi!
