⚡ 2-Qism: Future in the Past Decision Lab (15 ta Savol)
Qoida: was/were going to = o'tmishda qilinmoqchi bo'lgan, lekin ko'pincha amalga oshmagan niyat/reja. would = o'tmishda turib kelajak haqida berilgan va'da yoki bashorat (will ning o'tgan zamoni).
💡 Asosiy farq: "I was going to call you, but my phone battery died." (Niyat bor edi, uzilib qoldi). "He told me that he would love me forever." (O'tmishdagi va'da/kelajak).
🔄 3-Qism: O'tmish va Kelajak Zamonlar Transformatsiyasi (15 ta Savol)
Berilgan gaplarni o'tmish nutqiga (Reported Speech) o'giring. Zamonlar orqaga surilishi (Backshift: Present -> Past, Past -> Past Perfect, will -> would, is going to -> was going to) qoidalariga qat'iy e'tibor bering.
🔍 4-Qism: O'tmish va Kelajak Zamon Xatolarini Tuzatish (10 ta Qopqon)
O'tmish va kelajak zamonlar qoidalariga zid noto'g'ri jumlalar. Gapni to'liq to'g'rilab yozing.
🎯 5-Qism: Past vs Future Zamonlar Integratsiyasi (10 ta Test)
Past Simple, Past Continuous, Past Perfect, Future Continuous, Future Perfect va vaqt ergash gaplari bo'yicha to'g'ri variantni tanlang.
✍️ 6-Qism: Kontekstual Tarjima (8 ta Gap)
O'zbek tilidagi gaplarni o'tmish va kelajak zamon qoidalariga rioya qilgan holda inglizchaga tarjima qiling.
📝 7-Qism: Hikoya Yozish Labi: "The Day When Nothing Went According to Plan"
Boshlangan rejalaringiz buzilib ketgan yoki kutilmagan o'zgarishlar yuz bergan kun haqida hikoya yozing. Matnda kamida 3 ta 'was/were going to', 2 ta 'would' va 3 ta o'tmish zamoni (Past Continuous / Past Perfect) qatnashishi shart!
