Can, Could, Be Able To & 30 ta Noto'g'ri Fe'l (61–90)

B1 darajadagi qobiliyat, muvaffaqiyatli uddalash (was able to), kelajak/perfect shakllari hamda 61-dan 90-gacha bo'lgan 30 ta asosiy noto'g'ri fe'llar interaktiv trenajyori.

⚡ 1. CAN vs BE ABLE TO (Hozirgi vaqt)
Hozirgi zamonda ikkisi ham qobiliyatni bildiradi.
• 'Can' jonli nutqda ko'proq ishlatiladi: "I can speak English."
• 'Be able to' rasmiyroq: "She is able to solve complex equations."
🏆 2. COULD vs WAS/WERE ABLE TO (O'tmish)
Eng katta farq: Umumiy qobiliyat vs Bir martalik natija!
• Could: O'tmishda umumiy qila olgan ishlar ("I could swim when I was 5").
• Was/Were able to: Aniq bir qiyin vaziyatda bir martalik ishni uddalash ("The fire spread, but he was able to escape").
🔮 3. KELAJAK VA PERFECT ZAMONLAR
CAN fe'lining kelajak yoki perfect shakli yo'q!
• Kelajak: "I will be able to speak fluent English next year." (will can ❌)
• Present Perfect: "I haven't been able to sleep lately." (haven't can ❌)
🚫 4. INKORDA: COULDN'T vs WASN'T ABLE TO
Inkor gapda ikkalasi ham bir martalik holatda ham ishlayveradi!
• "I looked everywhere, but I couldn't find my keys." ✅
• "I looked everywhere, but I wasn't able to find my keys." ✅
⚠️ O'ZBEKCHA QOPQON (Eng ko'p uchraydigan xato):
O'zbek tilida "Men imtihondan o'ta oldim" va "Men yoshligimda tez yugura olardim" ikkalasi ham 'ola oldim/olardim'. Lekin ingliz tilida bir martalik qiyin vaziyatda maqsadga erishganda COULD deb bo'lmaydi! Masalan: "The exam was hard, but I could pass it" ❌ (XATO!) ➡️ "The exam was hard, but I was able to pass it" ✅ (TO'G'RI!).
